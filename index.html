Why this matters

Words matter. Actions matter.

Bullying, exclusion, gossip, and hostility hurt classroom relationships. Small acts of kindness help students feel safe, respected, and included.

Our message: Choose empathy over judgment. Choose respect over hostility. Choose to include others.

About us

A kinder classroom is possible.

Better Together is a student-led advocacy initiative promoting awareness about bullying and animosity among classmates. We encourage students to treat one another with respect, speak up against harmful behavior, and help create a welcoming school environment.

Our Mission

To raise awareness about bullying and encourage respect, empathy, and inclusion among students.

Our Vision

A school community where every student feels safe, accepted, and free to be themselves.

Our Values

Respect, kindness, empathy, accountability, and unity guide our advocacy.

Programs & resources

Support. Educate. Empower.

Our proposed programs encourage students to take part in building a respectful classroom community.

01

Awareness Campaign

Share posters and educational messages about bullying, its effects, and ways to prevent it.

02

Kindness Challenge

Encourage students to perform simple acts of kindness and include classmates in group activities.

03

Peer Support

Promote listening, empathy, and respectful conversations among classmates.

04

Educational Resources

Provide guidance on recognizing bullying, responding safely, and seeking help from trusted adults.

05

Conflict Resolution

Encourage calm discussions, accountability, and fair solutions to disagreements.

06

Speak Up Safely

Encourage students to report bullying to a teacher, school counselor, or trusted adult.

Remember

You are not alone.

If someone bullies you or a classmate, seek help from a trusted adult. You deserve to feel safe at school. Asking for support is a positive step.
